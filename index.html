About WRC

Building Future Innovators

Wellington Robotics Club is a community-based robotics organisation dedicated to inspiring young people through STEM education, engineering design and competitive robotics.

Our mission is to create an engaging environment where students can explore ideas, develop practical skills and grow into confident innovators and leaders.

Wonder

Curiosity, exploration and a lifelong love of learning.

Resilience

Challenges, iteration and the confidence to keep improving.

Creativity

Ideas, design thinking and innovative solutions.

Programmes

Learning Through Exploration, Creation and Innovation

Structured learning pathways help students develop technical skills, creative thinking and leadership through robotics and engineering.

01

Robotics Fundamentals

Hands-on building, mechanical design, sensors, teamwork and problem solving.

02

Coding & Engineering

Programming, automation, engineering design, testing and creative projects.

03

Competition Pathways

Preparation for RoboCup Junior, VEX IQ and future robotics challenges.

04

Leadership & Mentoring

Peer mentoring, communication, project leadership and community engagement.
